About PixelToCloud

A studio you can call.

Every small business we take on should be findable, able to scale, and able to convert. Pankaj Gupta and Rusmeen do the work themselves.

Start a projectContact us

How the studio works

Four stops, with the same two people on the work from the first call to the code in your name.

  1. 1

    Conversation

    You talk with Pankaj and Rusmeen. Offices in Gurugram, Haryana & Alwar, Rajasthan. Calls follow India time, including for clients in the US and the UAE.

  2. You approve

    • Pages and data
    • Milestone dates
    • What stays out of scope
    2

    Scope

    Pankaj leads systems, security, and infrastructure. The scope is written down, and you approve it before the build starts.

  3. 3

    Staging

    Rusmeen leads full-stack delivery. You review working software on a private link at each milestone.

  4. You receive

    • Repository
    • Domain
    • Server access
    4

    Handover

    You keep the repository, domain, and server credentials. A focused website launches in 7 days, or that payment is returned, with a 30-day warranty.
